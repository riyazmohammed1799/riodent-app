Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Governing Law: DPDP Act 2023 (India)
Executive Summary: RioDent connects dental clinics in Hyderabad with independent dental equipment technicians. We only collect the minimal clinic contact and address details required to dispatch a technician to your clinic. We do NOT collect patient health records, photos, videos, or financial payment details.

1. About RioDent & Service Area

RioDent ("we", "us", "our") is a specialized equipment maintenance dispatch platform for registered dental clinics and dental surgeons. Our current launch area covers the Hyderabad and Secunderabad metropolitan region in Telangana, India (PIN code prefixes: 500xxx, 501xxx, 502xxx).

2. Information We Collect

We adhere to the principle of strict data minimization under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. We collect only:

  • Clinic & Doctor Identity: Doctor/Dentist full name, dental clinic name, email address (for authentication via Google Sign-In or email/password).
  • Clinic Location & Contact: Practice mobile phone number (for technician coordination and status alerts), physical clinic address, landmark, area, pincode, and optional GPS coordinates supplied via device geocoding when booking.
  • Equipment Service Request Details: Equipment category (Dental Chair, Compressor, Autoclave, X-Ray / RVG, Suction, Scaler, Handpiece), description of malfunction, preferred visit date and time slot.
  • Service Completion Log: Date and time of service completion and the whole rupee amount paid directly by the clinic to the independent technician.

3. Information We Explicitly DO NOT Collect

  • No Dental Patient Health Data: We never collect, process, or store patient medical history, treatments, radiographs, or personally identifiable patient records. RioDent is purely for clinic equipment maintenance.
  • No Payment Card / Banking Credentials: The RioDent app is 100% free to use. Technicians are compensated directly in cash or UPI by the doctor at the clinic. We do not process or retain any payment credentials.
  • No Photo or Video Uploads: Media uploads and camera permissions are disabled in the MVP version.
  • No Continuous Background Tracking: Location access is only requested momentarily while you actively choose to detect your clinic address on the booking screen.

4. Purpose and Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your data exclusively for:

  • Dispatching and scheduling a qualified dental technician to your clinic address.
  • Sending visit confirmations, assignment updates, and status alerts via SMS, WhatsApp, or phone call.
  • Maintaining a digital maintenance and repair audit log for your clinic equipment.
  • Preventing fraudulent bookings and maintaining platform safety.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Sub-Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your clinic data. Data is shared strictly with:

  • Assigned Technicians: Once a service request is assigned, the assigned technician receives your clinic name, doctor name, clinic address, and mobile number to carry out the on-site visit.
  • Infrastructure Partners: Google Cloud & Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Hosting) under strict enterprise security contracts located in Google Cloud regions.

6. Data Retention and Security

We implement standard encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS 1.3) and encryption at rest. Clinic service tickets are retained for 1 year to allow doctors to review annual equipment service logs and tax receipts. Accounts and personal profiles are purged immediately upon account deletion.

7. Your Rights under DPDP Act 2023 & Play Store Guidelines

As a data principal, you have full control over your personal data:

  • Right to Access: View all your clinic details and service records directly inside the app.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit your doctor name, clinic name, mobile number, or address anytime via Profile settings.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You can delete your account and associated profile directly in-app (Profile → Delete My Account) or online without opening the app via our Public Account Deletion Page.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Contact our Grievance Officer for any privacy concerns.
