Request Account & Data Deletion

Submit a request to permanently delete your RioDent clinic account and associated records.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you have the RioDent app installed on your phone:

  1. Open the RioDent app.
  2. Tap the Clinic Profile icon in the top right corner.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm your password or re-authenticate to immediately erase your account.
Important Data Notice: Account deletion is permanent. Your doctor profile, clinic name, phone number, and authentication credentials will be erased immediately. Service ticket summaries are retained for up to 1 year solely for regulatory audit and clinic tax record compliance, after which they are purged.
